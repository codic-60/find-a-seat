# Find a Seat

Act as a pro web tool creator . scenario: It is the middle of the day. Once a class is over and students have free hours until their next lecture begins, they or their project groups need a quiet, free classroom to sit and work. Right now, they have to walk up and down three different floors, peeking into rooms, only to get kicked out 10 minutes later when a professor walks in for a scheduled lecture.   (scenario is for analysis and to build accordingly  )  .                                                                                                               Build a free class tracker that instantly tells students exactly where they can sit without being disturbed. You will use the same dataset of 10 class timetables to figure out where everyone is.                                                                                                                         Create a web tool to help students locate empty rooms efficiently using both traditional filters and AI text analysis.

The Floor Grid: Based on the 10 timetables provided, the website must figure out which rooms are currently empty at any given time. The results must be neatly organized floor-by-floor.

The AI Room Finder: Instead of forcing users to click through dropdown menus, integrate a smart text bar. A user should be able to type, "I need an AC room on the ground floor for me and my team for the next 2 hours." The AI must process this sentence, analyze the timetables, and spit out the exact rooms that match their specific needs and duration. I want web tool to be in a colour palette of black and purple . I want this tool in simple cool and interactive way . Use the fonts accordingly . Don't hallucinate .  ask any question u build before so that u won't waste tokens . get the timetable from the drive . fix any issue or errors while building.
